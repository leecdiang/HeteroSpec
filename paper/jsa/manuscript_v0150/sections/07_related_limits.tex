\section{Related Work and Limits}
\label{sec:limits}
\subsection{Relation to numerical-consistency methods}
Batch-invariant inference identifies reduction strategies for normalization, matrix multiplication and attention that avoid dependence on batch organization~\cite{thinkingmachines}. We do not claim the first observation that floating-point evaluation changes with geometry. Our design instead uses a particular query/mask view transformation and runtime phase/cache obligations in an existing quantized stack. The local mathematical mapping, sampled bitwise correspondence and full endpoint qualification remain different levels of evidence.

LLM-42 combines fast execution with fixed-shape verification, rollback and consistent state maintenance~\cite{llm42}. Consequently, fixed shapes, selective constraints and reuse of existing kernels are not independently novel claims here. Our evaluated candidate directly changes the target attention graph under a registered ordinary reference, without an extra numerical-consistency recovery replay. This distinction is architectural, not a demonstrated speed advantage over LLM-42. An adaptation in the historical experiments encountered reference-contract mismatches and incomplete confirmation; its results do not establish failure of the original system or provide a qualified same-reference ranking.

MarginGate uses margin-triggered selective verification~\cite{margingate}, whereas the present runtime applies a fixed, empirically qualified phase/layout recipe rather than a margin-based runtime controller. Vosti studies specification and verification of deterministic inference across system and kernel layers~\cite{vosti}; its guarantees should not be conflated with our finite output checks. Stage-Replay studies fixed-prefix numerical state and cache transplantation~\cite{stagereplay}. The B570 experiment adds a precision-fixed quantized-runtime partition intervention and a qualified boundary receiver, not a first general discovery that KV can carry numerical history.

The contribution therefore rests on the precise QB graph design and its in-stack same-reference implementation comparisons. Shared concepts in related work do not certify our method's portability, and different output contracts do not make external results invalid. A common-contract comparison with a mature external implementation remains open.

\subsection{Relation to speculation economics}
The original speculative decoding result preserves a target sampling distribution under its algorithmic assumptions~\cite{leviathan2023}; reproducing a chosen finite-precision greedy path is an additional implementation requirement. Production performance studies show why target verification cost and actual backend behavior matter beyond accepted-token counts~\cite{illusion,lossless}. We treat those insights as prior foundations. The new question here is the cost of alternative \emph{qualified repair implementations}, followed by the incremental cost of adding the speculative protocol on the repaired path.

The unrelated ACL work named HeteroSpec allocates verification using contextual heterogeneity~\cite{aclheterospec}. Our repository retains its historical project name, but this article uses a descriptive method title to avoid a naming collision. The scientific objectives and implementation contribution are distinct.

\subsection{Scope and threats to validity}
The structural mapping is reusable only when its stated tensor, stream, mask and head conditions hold. The numerical evidence is restricted to pinned non-flash CUDA graphs, small occupied query widths and the tested model conversions. Three families do not establish all architectures; shared backend components reduce their independence. Projection batching retained by QB may be inappropriate on another stack, as the Vulkan frontier illustrates. Neither operator replay nor mask enumeration supplies an all-input numerical-preservation theorem.

The complete-output studies use finite cap128 windows and greedy sampling. They do not establish stochastic distribution preservation for the implementation, long-context stability, concurrent throughput, energy efficiency, production tail latency or arbitrary state-restoration boundaries. Selected sensitive diagnostic inputs are appropriate for mechanism tests but cannot estimate the prevalence of divergence. Synthetic prefix experiments preserve exact native IDs, not natural task semantics.

Request medians and balanced order reduce some measurement variation but do not remove thermal, clock, operating-system or session confounding. Adjacent sessions cannot justify cross-date confidence intervals. The small PQ/QB advantages and task regressions should temper deployment claims; larger GS/QB differences do not remove the need for a fair local comparator. No fitted cost model or observer-overhead subtraction is used. Lack of continuous residency proof limits hardware-residency assertions rather than retroactively erasing the measured output and request observations.

Provenance failures are not silently repaired. The driver-freeze gap, rejected scale replays, resource amendments, missing historical slots and differing endpoint contracts remain explicit. The supplement preserves the historical evidence destinations and distinguishes reported-value checks in this reconstruction from original-host raw-data audits. Before public release, external readers need an accessible source/evidence package and a documented path to the exact model assets; a private repository pointer alone is not a public reproducibility guarantee.
