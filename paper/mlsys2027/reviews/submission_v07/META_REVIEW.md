## Shared findings and substantive disagreement

The new direct-ID studies establish a stronger qualification core: RTX 75/90
batched versus 90/90 serialized; B570 60/90 fast versus 78/90 serialized, against
a fixed 90/90 gate. Each preserves all 270 executions and 180 comparisons.
Direct generated-ID observations improve the earlier text-comparator weakness
prospectively. Historical timings remain their original evidence class.
Independent rigor replay supports the narrow endpoint; the reviews identify no
demonstrated integrity failure invalidating it. This does not attest chronology
or independently rehash omitted external assets.

The champion's case is substantive: an optimization space can disappear under
the actual output contract even when a local cost proxy looks favorable. A
characterization paper can earn acceptance through concrete, reproducible
negative controls. The two prospective studies transform an anecdotal historical
path-control observation into a bounded replicated qualification question.

The opposing case is also substantive. The reference-relative endpoint is sound,
but RTX and B570 differ in contexts, frontends and references. B570 reference
micro-batch 128 versus serialized candidate one leaves residual failures without
isolating serialized verification as their cause. One model pair and a short
cohort do not validate a transferable condition predicting qualification.
The historical CUDA diagnostic does not explain the new B570 failures. The
358H intervention still has no formal performance endpoint. No successful speed
remedy is mandatory for characterization, but the present explanatory increment
remains narrow under a selective general MLSys bar.
