\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
Favorable local indicators do not by themselves establish gains in integrated post-prefill decode on the tested consumer configurations. M4 exposes both exact positive and exact negative workload-conditioned regimes. Two measured failure modes clarify other outcomes: correctness changes the usable verification path, and an isolated draft advantage does not survive the measured stateful integration. These diagnoses are not exhaustive. The hardware conclusion is bounded to the frozen workloads, stacks, and post-prefill decode measurements, and stands without a new runtime result. The final paper conclusion remains provisional pending separately measured software evidence or removal of that reserved scope.
