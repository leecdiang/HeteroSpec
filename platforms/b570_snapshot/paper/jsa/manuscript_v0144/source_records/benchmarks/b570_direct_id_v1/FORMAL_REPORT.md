# B570 direct-generated-ID qualification replication

Prospectively frozen replication with known historical outcomes. 270/270 executions completed; no energy campaign.

- B570_FAST_K2: **BLOCKED**, 60/90 exact direct-ID pairs; 20/30 wholly exact prompts.
- B570_SERIALIZED_K2: **BLOCKED**, 78/90 exact direct-ID pairs; 26/30 wholly exact prompts.

Historical fast 45/90 and serialized 60/90 are re-tokenized-text agreement counts and remain unchanged. They are not direct generated-ID certificates.

Serialization improves agreement but does not establish qualification.

No Vulkan-wide or hardware-wide causal claim is made. No manuscript/figure edits or result-driven parameter search. All generated-ID comparisons and first-divergence evidence are in FORMAL_ANALYSIS.json.

## Direct-ID contract and mismatch evidence

The registered endpoint is the first 128 actual committed output IDs, or
the inclusive first runtime EOG earlier. Prompt IDs and uncommitted draft
candidates are excluded at the source instrumentation boundary. Entire raw
streams, including original CLI overshoot, are retained; no output string is
re-tokenized to establish equality.

- FAST: 30 mismatching pairs; first-divergence indices 1–124 (zero-based).
- SERIALIZED: 12 mismatching pairs; first-divergence indices 1–122.
- All three conditions reproduced the same direct-ID sequence across three
  repetitions for every one of the 30 prompts. Stability is not qualification:
  a stable candidate can still disagree with the reference.

Independent validation passed exact 270-run coverage and all 180 comparisons,
including pair counts, wholly exact prompt counts, raw sidecar/file hashes,
command templates, placement/offload evidence and first-divergence fields.
POST_RUN_INPUT_AUDIT.json records local rehashing of both model files and all
runtime binaries, the frozen dataset digest, and exact dataset-derived prompt
bytes. The portable bundle includes the frozen dataset for offline prompt
audit. Model weights and runtime binaries are excluded from the bundle; the
hardware-free validator checks their recorded hashes against protocol, not
bytes of omitted files.
