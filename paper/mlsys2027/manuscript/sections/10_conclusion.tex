\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
Favorable local indicators do not reliably translate into integrated speculative-decoding gains across the tested consumer configurations. M4 exposes both exact positive and exact negative workload-conditioned regimes. Two measured failure modes clarify other outcomes: correctness changes the usable verification path, and realized state-interface cost erases an isolated draft advantage. These diagnoses are not exhaustive. The hardware conclusion is bounded to the frozen workloads, stacks, and post-prefill decode measurements, and stands without a new runtime result. The final paper conclusion remains provisional pending separately measured software evidence or removal of that reserved scope.
