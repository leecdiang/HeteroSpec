## AI disclosure under the current policy

The [current Elsevier AI policy](https://www.elsevier.com/about/policies-and-standards/generative-ai-policies-for-journals) requires disclosure of AI-assisted manuscript preparation, including substantive sentence or organizational editing. Basic grammar, spelling and punctuation checks are exempt. A humanizer pass that rewrites prose belongs in the disclosure.

AI support for explanatory schematics is permitted with tool, version and use disclosed in each relevant caption and in the general declaration. AI-assisted data visualizations must faithfully derive from the underlying data through reproducible methods; Methods should name the tool/model, version and developer. AI use in research code or analysis should also be described in Methods. General-purpose generative image tools must not generate a graphical abstract.

The final declaration must reflect actual use and author review. Known current assistance includes manuscript editing, citation and consistency checking, and presentation work. Historical code/analysis tool identities and versions require confirmation. Do not invent tool versions or assert that final human review has already occurred. Author-side cross-checking with AI is not independent journal peer review.
