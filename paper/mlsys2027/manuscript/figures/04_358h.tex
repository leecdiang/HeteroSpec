Replay and proposal share one timed OpenVINO call; replay-only time is not measured. Initial prompt prefill is excluded. This diagnoses the tested runtime interface, not intrinsic NPU capability or a measured benefit from a state-persistent intervention. Together with Figure~\ref{fig:verification}, it identifies two measured reasons local opportunity need not become realized gain; the M4 contrast remains a separate empirical observation. CPU replay plus proposal is 12.63\%; NPU complete target work is 41.63\%. Residuals are 4.81 ms (0.04\%) and 5.06 ms (0.02\%). Prompt-GM speedup 95\% CIs are [0.693, 0.771] and [0.333, 0.369], respectively. The vertical dashed line is the shared arithmetic-mean baseline.
