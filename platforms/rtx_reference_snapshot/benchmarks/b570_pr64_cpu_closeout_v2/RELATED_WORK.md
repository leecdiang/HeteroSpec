# Direct primary-source comparison

Access: 2026-10-08. HTML abstract and selected design/evaluation sections were read unless explicitly marked abstract-only. Retrieval cache hashes are in SOURCE_BINDINGS.json. This is an author evidence review, not external peer review.

| Primary source / reading level | Closest existing principle | Relation and claim boundary |
|---|---|---|
| [LLM-42](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.17768v2), abstract + design | Verified speculation, deterministic verification and KV consistency | Fixed verifier paths and restoration are prior principles. B570 adds a finite native-runtime state/whole-recipe intervention and a qualified negative efficiency measurement; it adds no new accept/bonus algorithm. |
| [Vosti](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.38981v1), abstract + design | Formal contracts for deterministic inference and cache/execution behavior | Their verification framework is different evidence from our empirical greedy ID panels. We do not claim formal bitwise-logit equivalence or universal losslessness. |
| [Stage-Replay](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.28495v1), abstract + methods | Same integer prefix can carry different numeric KV; replay and cache transplantation | Numeric-state fidelity is directly related prior work. Natural native-runtime crossings and local repair/cost boundaries are empirical instances, not an invented cache-restoration principle. |
| [MarginGate](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.30218v1), abstract + design | Calibrated margin triggers and deterministic verification/cache repair | Our Q8/Vulkan history-dependent examples do not supply a calibrated online guard. A raw large margin cannot certify state fidelity; no cross-backend contradiction or universal trigger claim follows. |
| [Lossless but Not Free](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.17283), abstract + methods/evaluation | Distributional guarantees do not ensure consumer-hardware speed; paid components and numerical backend boundaries matter | Our work counts and ordinary-R negative comparator fit this existing distinction. We lack their kind of independently separated component timings and cannot invent a B570 duration breakdown. |
| [Speculative Decoding: Performance or Illusion?](https://proceedings.mlsys.org/paper_files/paper/2026/hash/554e056fe2b6d9fd27ffcd3367ae1267-Abstract-Conference.html), **abstract only** | Observed benefits vary with workload; verification cost and acceptance affect realizable gains | This level supports a broad motivation only. We make no assertion that the full paper omits state/path interaction or offers a directly applicable bound for B570. |
| [Batch-invariant inference](https://thinkingmachines.ai/blog/defeating-nondeterminism-in-llm-inference/), article/design | Fixed reductions and batch-independent numerical execution | Selective determinism and fixed numerical paths are existing principles. Our contribution must be the measured constraint scope and amortization boundary, not a rename of deterministic kernels. |

The nearest work already covers verification, numerical-state preservation and deterministic execution. A paper can claim scoped empirical diagnosis, tested local constraints and qualified cost comparisons; it cannot present these principles as a new automatic repair algorithm. Late mechanism data here predominantly concerns the same Qwen3 family; architecture-general claims remain unsupported.
