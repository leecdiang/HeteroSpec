## B570 results paragraph (integration-ready within the stated scope)

On Intel Arc B570 with the frozen Qwen3/Vulkan configuration and a 128-token endpoint contract, a fixed frontend combining ordinary-reference prompt construction, in-place prefill-to-continuation phase binding (UB128→UB1), and the existing k2/draftUB1 speculative loop recovered reference endpoints on 12 development requests, 112 requests over 56 previously exposed tasks, and all 30 candidate requests over 15 inputs previously unused in the accessible project. Endpoint equality was assessed through the first runtime EOG or token 128; it does not imply equality of the complete raw arrays, whose extra suffix work remained charged. Six natural-state crosses with 12 full own-path shams found local state–whole-recipe argmax interaction for HumanEval/145 and /42, and a state effect for MBPP/72 under duplicate current-recipe controls. These interventions establish finite numerical-state/path dependence, not a unique operator cause or repaired counterfactual trajectories. The formal E matrix remained 119/120 complete because one old-N1 request lacked payload. Ordinary R, matched target and candidate nevertheless each completed all 30 paid requests. Their task-median geometric-mean generation/whole speed ratios, defined as baseline time divided by candidate time, were 0.28259/0.20129 for R and 0.28576/0.20570 for matched target; the candidate was slower in every paired round. On 14 complete old-N1 task pairs it improved generation/whole time by 3.99309/2.63394, with one whole-request single-round reversal. Thus this finite repair is engineering progress without deployment gain over the ordinary reference, and does not justify further GPU investment in the present B570 route.

## Three candidate empirical contributions

1. **Natural-state crossing exposes finite state/path dependence beyond equal token prefixes.** Evidence: RTX retained-state crosses and B570 six full-float cases with byte-validated shams. Counterevidence/boundary: RTX current-layout argmax may stay fixed; B570 /72 has duplicate columns; crossings do not isolate a unique operator or certify a full repaired trajectory. Prior replay/KV work already establishes the general importance of numeric state.
2. **A concrete RTX local-constraint instance preserves finite references while retaining measured speculative gain.** Evidence: PR59/60 mask3 target prefill+continuation qualification and matched-target speed, PR62 expanded panel. Boundary: mask3 minimal only among tested switches; relaxing draft constraints does not meet its extra decode threshold; PR62 ordinary-R speed still requires the deferred CPU records. This is not automatic constraint discovery or global minimality.
3. **Output qualification and verification amortization must be jointly measured.** Evidence: qualified RTX gain vs matched target, fixed-UB wider-k slowdown, B570 scoped endpoint recovery yet strong-R slowdown. Counterevidence: RTX local gains are real and must stay; B570 matched target near R weakens the attribution of all candidate cost to consistency. No shared component timing model or hardware-wide impossibility result is established.
