\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
On the tested B570 stack, frozen draft-free UB1 predictions match 72/72 reserved capped sequences, including 12 runs of four reference-divergent prompts; reference/live agreement is 60/72. The test predicts complete future trajectories rather than only observing path sensitivity, but lossless compatibility remains blocked. Same-state forks examine intervention effects, and independently orchestrated RTX replay tests program reproduction. The cost comparisons lead to specific decisions: qualified RTX serialization is slower within its instrumented boundary; fixed-progress 358H target work prevents parity even at zero draft/residual cost; M4 Guard loses to fixed speculation despite preserving IDs. Each conclusion is limited to its tested reference and execution path. No universal mechanism or successful portable optimization is established.
