\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
Controlled output prediction, reference restoration and optimization produce different verdicts in the tested speculative-decoding stacks. B570's intervention restores its exposed cohort; RTX's predicts correctly while repairing one task and introducing another deviation. A fresh matched study shows that ordinary target-only execution already reproduces the RTX intervention on all 19 tested tasks. Offline selection adds no demonstrated advantage over simple baselines. Separate measurements show qualified RTX slowdown, conditional 358H limits and M4 control losing to fixed speculation. Together these results support explicit output contracts, paired effects and complete cost accounting, while narrowing claims of general prediction, repair and control capability.
