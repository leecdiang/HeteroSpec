\section{Discussion and Related Work}
\label{sec:related}
\subsection{Preserving numerical behavior during optimization}
Batch-invariant inference controls reduction choices in normalization, matrix multiplication and attention~\cite{thinkingmachines}. Taming reconstructs the bitwise arithmetic of existing cuBLAS kernels, preserves the recovered reduction descriptions during lowering and verifies properties of the compiled arithmetic~\cite{taming}. These works show how a numerical behavior can guide optimization. Our native experiments identify the additional execution dependencies that arise when known-query work is reorganized in a quantized runtime: the physical attention range, backend-specific arithmetic and placement, and the producer/state boundary.

The small-column quantized path is an existing implementation technique, including in public \texttt{llama\_reproducible} code~\cite{loop4code}. We retain backend-loop4 as a direct performance control. The packing experiment asks what remains to be gained by exposing its independent groups to the GPU; the factorial design separates that change from attention-local cuts. The mixed-Q4 extension applies the same construction with scalar-column inner arithmetic and its own stated control.

\subsection{Deterministic engines and speculation}
Vosti relates engine state and cache behavior to kernel properties through formal determinism contracts~\cite{vosti}. LLM-42 combines a consistent verifier execution with verification and state repair~\cite{llm42}. CoRun isolates prefill and uses fixed-shape batched decode for deterministic service~\cite{corun}. Our starting point is the execution of a deployment fixed before optimization. Source qualification identifies its arithmetic, and live metadata determines compatible physical ranges and phase transitions. This permits both whole-graph cuts and attention-local cuts within a wider graph.

Speculative decoding and sampling reduce target invocations by verifying proposed tokens together~\cite{leviathan2023,chen2023}. Their distributional arguments and our numerical target address different properties: the latter compares full finite-precision vectors and active state on the evaluated prefixes. HeteroSpec uses contextual heterogeneity to allocate verification effort~\cite{liu2026heterospec}. Proposal policy remains an application choice here; reference-compatible execution supplies the target work that the policy requests.

\subsection{Scope and deployment choices}
The implementation covers a dense, nonwrapping cache, one sequence, F16 KV and non-Flash attention. Its guards make those assumptions explicit. Paged or wrapped caches, additional KV types and fused attention require corresponding geometry extraction and native arithmetic recipes. The two backends already illustrate why the recipe is hardware-specific: identical logical tensors can invoke different activation, reduction and placement choices.

Within this scope, Whole and Local offer different amounts of outer-graph parallel work while satisfying the same numerical target. Their relative cost depends on how often a request encounters incompatible extents and how much work lies outside the cuts. Packing has its own cost contribution. The complete-request experiments then account for draft computation, target verification, output handling and state maintenance. This separation lets the evaluation identify a compatible implementation's incremental benefit and the application configuration that makes useful work from it.
