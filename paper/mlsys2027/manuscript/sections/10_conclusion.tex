\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
Under the tested fixed stacks, frozen execution-path descriptions predict complete native generated IDs and endpoints before and after two controlled interventions. B570 restores reference agreement on its exposed diagnostic/control cohort; RTX predicts the changed paths but fails its predefined recovery and no-new-regression target. Equal RTX aggregate agreement conceals one restoration and one new deviation. The studies validate distinct descriptions and configurations, not a universal repair or stage cause. Qualification and cost then support separate decisions: historical RTX serialization is compatible but slower, 358H overhead-only ceilings remain below parity, and M4 Guard loses to fixed speculation. Predict the realized path, evaluate its reference compatibility, and measure its actual cost before calling it an optimization.
