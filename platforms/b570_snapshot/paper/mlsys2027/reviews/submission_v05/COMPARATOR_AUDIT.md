## Decision

**P0 confirmed: historical M4, RTX HumanEval, and B570 comparisons use IDs obtained by re-tokenizing CLI text, not IDs captured from generation.** The M4 72/72 and 90/90 results, the RTX serialized 90/90 results per placement, and B570 45/90 and 60/90 counts remain valid statements of their recorded comparator outcomes. They must not be presented as evidence that the directly generated token sequences are equal. This is a correction to the scope of inference, not evidence of additional generation failures and not a reason to revise frozen numbers or evidence classes.

The prospective M4 runtime study has a separate direct-ID implementation and retains its 48/48 qualification and **180/180 direct-ID formal comparisons for each of Always-Spec and Guard**. It does not retroactively establish direct-ID equality in the earlier studies. The 358H heterogeneous runner also accumulates committed IDs directly; this audit supplies no basis for extending the text-path limitation to its completed Phase-3 results or to separately captured CUDA token/score diagnostics.

## Required claim fixes

### Section 2 must define two evidence scopes

Initial `manuscript/sections/02_framework.tex:6–11` gives a direct generated-ID contract, says admissible paths pass “that contract,” and then calls historical RTX controls exact. Merely adding “intended” before “contract” leaves the logical promotion at line 8 intact. Replace or qualify the admissibility rule in the same subsection, not only in Limitations. Recommended concrete text:

> Our strict output-preservation target is equality of directly generated token IDs against the matched target-only greedy output. The frozen campaigns do not all observe this target through the same comparator. Historical M4 and RTX HumanEval runners compare re-tokenized parsed CLI text; the HumanEval parser includes displayed prompt text. B570 compares re-tokenized, prompt-stripped text normalized to the runtime output count and cap. We retain their reported comparator counts and evidence classes as historical text-path evidence; these counts alone do not establish direct generated-ID equality. The 358H heterogeneous runner and the prospective M4 runtime study compare committed/generated IDs directly. Qualification and admissibility below are scoped to the stated comparator, workload, and execution path; strict direct-ID claims use the direct comparator.

If “admissible” is reserved exclusively for strict direct-ID qualification, historical paths instead need the explicit label “passed the historical text-path comparator” throughout. Either vocabulary is consistent; treating every historical `qualified` label as a direct-ID pass is not. Do not silently relabel historical PRIMARY/SECONDARY classes as diagnostic to resolve this wording conflict.
