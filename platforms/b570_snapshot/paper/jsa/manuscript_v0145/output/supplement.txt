Abstract
The supplemental evidence for manuscript v0.14.5 covers output contracts, implementation boundaries, campaign-level results,
negative findings, historical measurements and missing observations. Task panels and timer conventions remain distinct. The author
source package reproduces figures and arithmetic from committed exports without model inference. Verification covers inspected
sources and exports, with inherited original-host raw audits identified separately. Sections S1–S7 are numbered independently of the
main manuscript.
