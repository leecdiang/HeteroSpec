\section{Evidence contracts and campaign identities}
\label{supp:contracts}
\subsection{A layered evidentiary interpretation}
An exact tensor indexing argument establishes the mathematical meaning of the layout, not numerical determinism. Fixed-input replay establishes agreement or disagreement of recorded operators under identical operands, not all model states. Full native endpoint qualification establishes the chosen response property over a finite panel, not task-answer accuracy or all future responses. A timing comparison additionally requires complete equivalent-output pairs and a common paid interval. A causal state experiment additionally requires valid control of the imported and reconstructed state. These predicates are intentionally not collapsed into a single ``pass'' count.

The native endpoint in the new principal campaigns includes the entire generated ID array, per-ID EOG flags, array length, last ID, stop reason and registered cap. The cap is 128, so a task reaching that bound is not evidence of correctness or consistency beyond it. Older B570 endpoints sometimes ended at the first EOG while raw returned arrays continued. Those extra IDs remained charged, and endpoint equality did not certify full-array equality. Re-tokenized text in the earliest platform studies is weaker still. Later direct-ID qualification cannot retroactively certify those historical costs.

Each campaign has its own frozen model, frontend, numerical libraries, prompt formatting and reference. A common family label does not make two converted model files interchangeable. CUDA Q8\_0 target/draft pairs and Vulkan Q6\_K/Q4\_K\_M pairs must not be pooled as one hardware comparison. Current exact converted files are bound by the original host's hashes; public publisher conversion bytes are distinct from certified official checkpoint ancestry.

\subsection{Frozen versus exposed inputs}
The larger CUDA confirmation uses 24 inputs frozen against accessible execution history. The 12-task repair-comparison panel is deliberately exposed and supports a within-panel implementation comparison. The numerical replay uses 12 exposed tasks, two sampled contexts each, and only the indicated layer-0 operators. A context is a native observation boundary, not a new model, a new tensor shape, or an independently sampled benchmark task. Some early prefill operands coincide because tasks share a prompt prefix; repeated agreement should not be promoted to additional geometry coverage.

The B570 causal inputs include two known divergent GSM8K cases, two known stable long controls, and three synthetic prefixes of one input. Selecting sensitive cases is appropriate for testing a mechanism, but does not estimate failure prevalence. The stable controls are retained to distinguish numerical difference from a changed output. The fixed-prefix cases do not carry benchmark-answer correctness claims. All task identities and exact native-ID sources remain separate from the reported latency panel.

\subsection{Actual compilation and observation scopes}
CUDA uses a pinned GGML/llama.cpp-derived frontend with non-flash attention, full GPU placement requested, F16 KV and native greedy generation~\cite{llamacpp}. The CUDA study build uses the b10839/\kw{0cae43063} build identity with study-specific patches. The Vulkan source and binaries are hash-pinned, but their upstream ancestry was not certified; its exact local identities remain in the companion records. The repair comparison uses target caller UB4 and draft UB1 at $k=3$; GS changes physical target microbatching without changing result capacity or speculative reconciliation. B570's causal target-only actor uses UB512 or UB128 as registered, capacity 1, context 1024, logical batch 512 and single-query continuation. Actual placement and operational budget checks are distinct from continuous physical residency. No new result claims the latter.
