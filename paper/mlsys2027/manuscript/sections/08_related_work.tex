\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}
\paragraph{Performance and cost accounting.}
Speculative decoding amortizes target computation while preserving a target sampling distribution under its algorithmic assumptions \citep{leviathan2023}. \citet{illusion} systematically study speculative methods in vLLM, including verification dominance and workload/batch effects. \citet{specllm} control shared runtime policies and express speedup through committed work and conditional execution cost. \citet{distance} derive profitability conditions for edge--cloud execution. TurboSpec profiles execution and uses feedback to adjust or disable speculation for serving goodput \citep{turbospec}. BanditSpec selects speculative configurations online and analyzes stopping-time regret \citep{banditspec}; our oracle ratio is a descriptive latency comparison, not a regret guarantee. Our retrospective case studies examine two measured boundaries of integrated post-prefill decode: which verification paths remain usable after correctness qualification, and whether isolated draft throughput carries through the actual state interface. Our prospective M4 study adds a frozen one-way control experiment on a correctness-qualified consumer path. It is not a comparison against serving controllers or a claim that adaptive speculation or cost/commit accounting is new.

\paragraph{Heterogeneous execution.}
Dovetail places drafting on a GPU and target verification on a CPU \citep{dovetail}; DuoDecoding studies CPU/GPU placement with hardware-aware draft budgets \citep{duodecoding}. Mirror-SD explicitly considers GPU/NPU mapping and complementary speculative pipelines \citep{mirror}. Heterogeneous speculative execution is therefore established prior art. Our negative heterogeneous outcome characterizes the qualified interface and does not contradict acceleration under other placements or runtimes.

\paragraph{Correctness and numerical reproducibility.}
\citet{batchcorrect} analyze batch speculative-decoding synchronization errors and distinguish them from residual numerical mismatches. Their paper revises the earlier \emph{Batch Speculative Decoding Done Right} preprint. \citet{numerics} study numerical sources of output variation across system configurations; LLM-42 uses verified speculation to enforce deterministic inference \citep{llm42}. Our CUDA observation is not a new numerical phenomenon, and we do not equate it with ragged-batch synchronization bugs. Our systems contribution is prospective direct-ID path qualification under two consumer GPU runtimes: serialization changes the set of paths admitted by a frozen output contract, qualifying the tested RTX path but leaving B570 blocked. This complements numerical/deterministic inference studies without claiming firstness, a new batch-shape phenomenon, or a shared root cause.

\paragraph{State and memory reuse.}
Draft-side state management also has prior art. \citet{compactmemory} maintain incrementally updated compressed draft KV memory for long contexts. Such designs differ from the full-prefix replay interface measured here, but preclude treating state reuse itself as a new idea. Our evidence identifies a realized replay-plus-proposal cost in a consumer OpenVINO NPU path. The subsequent 358H runtime study stops at qualification, while M4 evaluates cost control on an available exact path. Neither result establishes a measured benefit from persistent draft state.
