\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
Frozen B570 target-only predictions match 72/72 reserved capped trajectories, including four non-reference prompts; the reference matches 60/72 and remains blocked. A separate intervention changes scores but not sampled immediate decisions. RTX discriminates predefined descriptions: target-only UB2 matches 51/57, replay 57/57, with distinct UB2/reference failures. These results constrain predictive descriptions without identifying necessary history or a unique numerical cause. Matched RTX cost then rejects instrumented serialization as an optimization; 358H requires changing the fixed progress/target-work premise to reach parity through cost savings; M4 Guard loses to fixed speculation. Prediction, reference compatibility and complete-cost opportunity provide different information, and each must be tested on its own stated path.
