# Adversarial review — v0.8 (internal model-review simulation)

Review target: the actual v0.8 main PDF and appendix, with the B570 closeout as evidence. **Assessment: Weak Reject, confidence 3/5.** The 72/72 reserved prediction is real progress and must not be dismissed as merely “test the actual path.” It is a prospective, complete-sequence prediction after H1 and prediction freezes; four residual divergent prompts make the result more discriminating than an all-equal endpoint.

My strongest objection is the remaining jump from a controlled within-stack prediction to a broadly consequential systems contribution. The discovery and reserved sets share HumanEval, model pair, build, output cap, and B570 numerical environment. Predicting new prompts on that frozen configuration is useful but does not demonstrate a transferable diagnostic across a second stack or a change in runtime choice. The paper's other results do not supply that transfer: RTX has no H1 test, 358H is a separate state-interface failure, and M4 Guard has negative incremental control value. These limits are honestly stated, but a selective committee might still judge the contribution narrow.

The H1 comparison isolates a sufficient path, not unique causation. Any claim that micro-batch one is a general correction or that B570 is defective would be unsupported; the PDF avoids both. Historical text comparators also remain weaker than direct generated-ID evidence, and the fresh qualification cannot convert old timing into exact-path performance. I find the appendix exposure caveat appropriate but cannot independently exclude erased/unavailable local traces.

The v0.7 objection is **partly resolved**: the work now has a controlled explanatory and predictive result in B570. The remaining blocker is scientific consequence outside this frozen configuration, not the absence of a falsifiable test. I would not demand universal transfer as the only acceptable evidence, but I would ask the committee to weigh whether this within-stack consequence clears its systems bar.
