\section{CPU Reconstruction, Provenance, and Access Limits}
\label{supp:reproducibility}
The author source package provides editable LaTeX tables/equations, separate vector PDF/SVG figures, bibliography, Highlights, CPU reconstruction and build code, selected authenticated report/implementation records, claim--evidence mapping and the complete experiment-destination ledger. It includes the retained Elsevier class and numeric bibliography style. The two document entry points are \texttt{main.tex} and \texttt{supplement.tex}. Compile either directly in Overleaf; the documented local build regenerates tables and figures from the frozen exports before compilation. A separately rebuilt flat source package addresses Elsevier's submission-source layout; source compilation does not establish acceptance by Editorial Manager.

The evidence bindings and historical source copies inherited from v0.14.0 are preserved. The included CPU verifiers reconstruct the RTX 384-row matrix, qualification flags and response-ID digest bindings, medians, all 12 comparisons, descriptive intervals, task regressions, round reversal and ratio identities. They reconstruct B570's 119 formal rows, 15 versus 14 complete task pairs, all three cost timers and paid suffix counts. They also check the phase task booleans, natural-state matrices, fixed-UB task-ratio aggregation and M4 medians/oracle. These are calculations from compact exports, distinct from a renewed audit of every original raw payload.

The original runtime reports contain stronger original-host audits, retained external-asset hashes and large content-addressed archives. Those audits are inherited evidence. The author-side reconstruction reads selected reports and source, checks copied-source bindings and recomputes included exports. It does not reconstruct every private archive, locally rehash omitted model/driver binaries, verify independent absolute execution time or run new GPU experiments. Numerical reproduction of a summary or task-ratio export must not be called a full raw-data audit. Missing B570 payloads and unknown suffix comparisons remain unknown.

The v0.14.2 validation records identify the checks actually executed for this revision: CPU inputs and outputs, preserved-file scope, clean compilation, source-package extraction and rebuilding, PDF hashes and build metadata, and the final pages inspected visually. Earlier numerical and visual validation records remain attributed to their original versions. The records distinguish the Overleaf package from the flat submission-source package; Editorial Manager's signed-in upload and acceptance remain author actions. These are author-side validation activities, not external peer review or commercial plagiarism checking.

HumanEval, MBPP and Spec-Bench source descriptions are cited in the main manuscript~\cite{humaneval,mbpp,specbench}; software is cited directly~\cite{llamacpp,qwen3}. Web/preprint versions and access dates are recorded in the bibliography and reference audit. Authors must finalize authorship, affiliations, CRediT, funding, competing interests, permissions, permanent data/software access and the AI-assistance statement before submission. A second model family, external method implementation comparisons, stochastic sampling and concurrent throughput are outside the completed evidence scope; no future outcomes are supplied here.
