Artifact-only decision using the v0.8.3 main and appendix PDFs, six independent reports, consolidated revisions, and MLSys-style meta-review.

**0.** The reported within-stack 72/72 prediction is bounded and falsifiable; the PDF-only inability to replay full arrays or independently timestamp the freeze is an audit limit, not demonstrated falsity. The two weak rejections center on significance and transfer.

## B. Shared P1 writing and positioning issues

**4:** CR-01 (24 prompts/four informative divergences next to 72/72), CR-02 (full-array and temporal audit access or precise disclosure), CR-03 (predictive rather than unique causal sufficiency), and CR-07 (historical text-path timings versus fresh ID gates). See `CONSOLIDATED_REVISIONS.md` for exact reviewer counts and locations.

## C. Would experiments change validity or broaden scope?

- A separately frozen second-backend/model prediction would probe **transfer and systems significance**, and could change R2/R4's rejection judgments. It is not needed to validate the present B570 configuration's reported finite-set outcome.
- A history-controlled live target-partition switch would test a **stronger causal mechanism** than claimed. Fresh matched RTX latency would establish a **new joint cost/qualification endpoint**. Neither can be inferred from the existing PDF.
- Anonymous full-array and freeze-event access would improve **independent verifiability of existing evidence**, not add model inference. If those records are absent or inconsistent, the central claim must be revisited rather than smoothed by text.

## D. Can the paper be submitted after textual changes only?

**Yes for the scoped weak-accept judgment.** Move the actual 24-prompt/four-divergence granularity and same-set 60/72 versus 72/72 contrast beside the main claim; disclose PDF-only audit limits; tighten predictive/causal language and cross-campaign labels. Reviewer-accessible evidence from existing records would be better if feasible, subject to anonymity. None of these steps requires a new experiment.

## Final classification

**SUBMIT AFTER MATERIAL TEXTUAL REVISION.** The AC is WEAK ACCEPT at 4/5 confidence; the independent reviewers are four accept-side versus two weak rejects. The latter expose a real venue-significance dispute that text can calibrate but cannot erase. Cross-backend transfer remains optional for the current claim and may be the most consequential route to a firmer decision.
