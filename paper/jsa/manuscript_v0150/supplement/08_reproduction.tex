\section{Reproduction, Disclosure and Claim Audit}
\label{supp:reproduction}
\subsection{What this author package can reproduce locally}
The author package contains complete LaTeX sources for the new article and supplement, editable vector figure definitions, the 21-cell repair comparison dataset, evidence-scope JSON, the 28-group disposition CSV, source pointers, a build script and a reported-data checker. Building does not invoke a model, require a GPU, or change the original repository. The checker verifies declared request arithmetic, all 21 table entries, task-regression denominators, rounded ratio identities, principal qualitative conclusions, and critical limitation statements. It does not pretend to reconstruct individual request medians from a table of aggregate ratios.

The original-host reports describe stronger raw-data procedures: native-result reconciliation, captured-tensor byte checks, independent standard-library audits, synthetic corrupted-evidence rejection tests, and fresh local/authenticated GitHub archive restoration. These remain attributed to their respective source records. For example, the final B570 report records 15 malformed-evidence controls and remote reconstruction of 10 new artifacts plus five PR99 artifacts. Merely copying their PASS fields would not constitute repeating those audits on a new machine.

\subsection{Scope of the new claim map}
Every main claim has an explicit source group, evidence level and forbidden overstatement in \kw{CLAIM\_EVIDENCE.md}. The address-mapping proposition is a mathematical derivation of the declared indexing assumptions. CUDA bitwise correspondence is empirical and sampled. Three-family qualification is complete only over the registered finite endpoints. Repair cost is a same-reference whole-request comparison. B570 KV sensitivity is conditional on a validated boundary receiver. None substitutes for the others.

Some source metadata and ledger wording are historical. The old statement that repair evidence covers only Qwen3 is superseded by the later confirmation, not deleted from the archived version. Conversely, a later stronger causal experiment does not alter the original failed factorial or change its admissible wording. The older stock-reference failure remains visible even when a different post-hoc pairing has equal output. The paper therefore uses separate source identities rather than rewriting all experimental history around the latest successful result.

\subsection{Independent scrutiny still needed before submission}
Before submission, the author should review the full mathematical lowering against the exact runtime source, the PQ metadata-capacity correction, the qualified/failed operator replay boundary, and the complete-state receiver contract. An independent reader should be able to obtain the evidence needed to reproduce each headline from raw records. If access to the private source/capture capsules cannot be provided, the data-availability statement must disclose the limitation rather than advertise public reproducibility.

The final funding, competing-interest, author-contribution and AI-use statements require author approval. The descriptive paper title avoids collision with the unrelated ACL HeteroSpec publication~\cite{aclheterospec}. A final bibliography/version check and journal-format review should precede upload. No journal submission, arXiv update, model experiment or PR merge was performed as part of this manuscript reconstruction.
