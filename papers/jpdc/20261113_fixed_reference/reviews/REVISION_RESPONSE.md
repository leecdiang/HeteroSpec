## Remaining scientific and author decisions

The method remains a specialized compatibility construction with finite deployment-specific qualification. Its contribution relative to existing arithmetic/scheduling and state-contract work remains a substantive novelty judgment. Workload diversity is limited and several task identities are reused. Full default modern-backend service is unmeasured. The present process-exit boundary is not a general persistent-service transaction recovery implementation. Restricted and pruned original native records limit complete public replay.

Those limits are explicit in the manuscript and do not call for inventing experiments. Broader claims would require new implementation or evidence under a separate authorization. Final author identity, CRediT, funding, interests, acknowledgments, rights, archival data/access terms, historical AI use and approval of the manuscript remain unresolved author decisions.
