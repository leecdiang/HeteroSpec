\section{Reproduction and evidence access}
\label{supp:reproduction}
A single \texttt{main.tex} builds the article and supplement with a shared bibliography, continuous page numbers and editable TikZ/PGFPlots figures. The arXiv source includes complete reported cost and state matrices, plotting values, the original byte-verified RTX tables, the later service blocks and standard-library checkers. The checker reconstructs the E2 point estimates and registered bootstrap intervals and verifies report-level arithmetic, source links, table identities and LaTeX dependencies. It performs no model execution or full tensor replay.

\texttt{anc/source\_manifest.json} pins every underlying campaign to an exact commit and records access/reproduction scope. The original RTX evidence is sealed at \texttt{f997262d} and the historical B570 support audit at \texttt{9fafc4ba}. Later RTX service records are \texttt{9868acfb}; the B570 formal service and policy records are \texttt{51febadd} and \texttt{e44c4df0}. The separate scope of each is retained in the follow-up index. Repository pointers do not guarantee public access to model weights, runtime capsules or full tensor archives. The arXiv package makes the paper's numerical tables and analysis inputs available independently of those larger dependencies.

Detailed development history is preserved in \texttt{anc/historical\_disposition.csv} and \texttt{anc/editorial\_archive/}: all 28 legacy groups, later campaign dispositions, failed acquisitions, original session sensitivity and operation-level accounting. Moving them outside the PDF changes presentation, not their outcome or eligibility. Failed comparisons that constrain current claims remain in this supplement.

The bounded AV architecture exploration is archived separately at \texttt{dae5aea9}. It uses a declared F32 arithmetic reference, 64-key/two-channel operand subviews and five names for one policy; its 390-cell parity is a consistency control, not native AV replay or a hardware advantage. The later query/head, scoped GQA and finite-panel service results are included in Sections~\ref{sec:mappingcosts}--\ref{sec:service}; prospective or incomplete variants remain identified as such. No new-input population claim is added. This revision changes the manuscript and its analysis package only; it adds no native inference or hardware experiment.

The source package preserves the original claim matrices, bibliography keys and report-level inputs. Editable figures and tables accompany the saved data. The repair--draft interaction is reconstructed from the existing seven-arm ratios; its script records the formulas and reporting-rounding bounds separately from sampling uncertainty. Generated TeX tables reside in \texttt{tables/}, outside the ancillary-data directory, so article compilation is independent of ancillary-file handling.
