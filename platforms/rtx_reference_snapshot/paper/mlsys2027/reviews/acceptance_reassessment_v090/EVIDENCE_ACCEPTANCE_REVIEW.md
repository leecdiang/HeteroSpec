# Independent evidence and inference review — v0.9.0

**Recommendation: 3/6, weak reject. Confidence: 4/5.** This is an internal MLSys research-track recommendation, not a conference decision or acceptance probability. I formed the judgment independently from the frozen packet and primary public sources. The decisive concern is the size and transferability of the scientific contribution, not a demonstrated numerical failure of the bounded claims.

The submission establishes unusually carefully scoped finite facts: a simpler B570 target-only program predicts complete native capped outputs, specified RTX target-only programs have distinct failures, proposal-conditioned replay succeeds, and separately qualified cost experiments rule out several measured optimization directions. My direct arithmetic agrees with the central results. The paper does not establish a general predictor, a discriminating causal explanation, a validated correction, or measured diagnostic benefit. Under the MLSys research criteria of novelty, quality, interest, and impact, I regard the technical care as strong, interest as moderate, and the incremental research insight and demonstrated practical impact as below the acceptance threshold. A weak accept is defensible if the committee places substantial weight on rigorous empirical reproducer selection and transparent negative results; my assessment gives greater weight to what another investigator can infer or do beyond these frozen cases.

## What the evidence establishes

**B570 H1 is a meaningful finite simplification.** Elementwise comparison of all 72 retained rows gives prediction/live 72/72 and reference/live 60/72. All three conditions are stable within every prompt and every array has 128 IDs. There are 24 prompt units, not 72 independent prompts. The four distinct reference-divergent trajectories have first differences at indices 88, 73, 103, and 93. They contain respectively 40, 55, 25, and 35 differing positions through cap: 155 total, of which 151 are strictly after the first differences. These are correlated trajectory extents, not independent token trials. The complete suffix forecast is stronger evidence than predicting only a mismatch flag or first argmax. It supplies an empirically sufficient draft-free reproducer for this stack and cohort. It does not qualify native speculation against UB128, prove drafting causally irrelevant inside native execution, or establish an uncapped guarantee.

**The B570 fork limits attribution rather than resolving it.** From all 72 retained forks I recover 216 paired raw-score rows and 195 reachable paired decisions. Scores change in all runs, raw top-one IDs change in zero rows, and immediate committed/acceptance decisions change in zero events. Median absolute top-one score change is 0.06954384, p95 0.34370041, and maximum 2.35025787; minimum retained raw margins are 0.22905731 and 0.20034409. These marginal descriptors do not certify a uniform perturbation bound or supply a near-tie decision witness. Complete continuations differ on 18 runs from six prompts, 10–50 positions after the fork. Because later UB128 execution persists and proposal history evolves, this is not a controlled single-block delayed effect, an immediate restoration, or an explanation of the earlier H1 cohort. The paper explicitly recognizes those limits. Exported state-check booleans support the reported checks but are not raw KV or opaque sampler bytes that I independently compared.

**RTX V4 discriminates programs, not a necessity theorem.** I compare all 285 A arrays and find every condition repeat-stable. Target-only UB2 and the UB1 reference each match 51/57, while H2 and fresh R_ONLY each match 57/57. Their equal totals conceal different failures: /25 has one UB2 error at index 4 and then reconverges; /15 defeats both target-only programs, with ten differing positions on the 25-ID live extent plus three extra IDs; /18 defeats UB1, with forty differences over 69 live positions and three extra IDs. Hindsight choice between the two target-only complete arrays covers 18/19 prompts and is not a predictor. All retained A/B endpoints satisfy the inclusive-first-EOG or cap rule.

The 19 prompts are all remaining exposure-audited tasks, not a representative sample; 57 repeats provide stability evidence, not 57 independent tasks. A and B share this cohort, and B uses fresh runs rather than a new unseen prediction sample. H2 proposals use the program's own earlier target feedback, and fresh R_ONLY computes target decisions while reading a sealed proposal tape. Tape construction still uses a draft. Numerical routines are shared with native execution. UB2 is a capacity-level alternative whose actual occupied forward shapes and KV history are not established equal to native speculation. Its failure validly rejects that program; replay success does not demonstrate necessary proposal/history input or a minimal state representation. I also recover the earlier discovery result 24/36 on 12 prompts and V2 replay 72/72 versus reference 60/72 on its separate 24 prompts. I do not pool these cohorts.

**Fresh RTX B supports the measured serialization exclusion.** From all 171 B rows I reconstruct QPC durations, reference/candidate ID gates, balanced scheduling, and condition medians. Batching is blocked at 51/57; serialization qualifies at 57/57 with stable reference repeats. The prompt GM of target/serialized median ratios is 0.6773510334393157, its reciprocal relative GM latency is 1.476339372987154, and serialization is faster on 0/19 prompts. Each condition occupies every order position 19 times. The independently recovered absolute distributions agree with the tables; dividing their distribution medians would be a different estimator. I did not rerun the bootstrap, so the CI is reviewed as reported, not independently recalculated.

This conclusion applies to the instrumented timer after synchronized target/draft prefill, including request sampler/state work, generation, reconciliation, logging/response, and slot release. Loading, initial prefill, and teardown are excluded. Clock probes bound clock-call overhead, not all logging cost; observer controls check outputs, not logging-free latency. Global micro-batch changes also propagate to draft. These facts justify a whole-path instrumented configuration comparison, not a target-only causal intervention or deployment-speed estimate. M4 instead starts after sampler/speculator setup and the speculative begin hook; 358H charges its complete post-prefill runner loop. The different boundaries and energy domains cannot support cross-platform rankings or component subtraction.

**358H arithmetic is estimator-consistent and conditional.** I inspect all 180 processed CSV rows, verify positive baseline/target time, nonnegative other regions and component sums, and recover 11,520 committed IDs per path, 4,677/4,686 rounds, and C = 2.46311738/2.45838668. Correction/bonus output belongs in this progress denominator; accepted-draft-only counters would be wrong. Actual prompt GMs are 0.73391459/0.35166324, whereas pooled total-time ratios are 0.72266816/0.34772322.

At fixed matched baseline, target work, and output progress, zero draft/residual cost gives pooled ceilings 0.82745298/0.83518585. The original median-of-three-paired-ratios then prompt-GM estimator has separate ceilings 0.8372192092432174/0.8448401086171792. Monotonicity of the median and GM makes this bound valid. Necessary uniform target-work cuts under zero other cost are therefore 16.278079%/15.515989%, not the different pooled thresholds. No prompt-median ceiling reaches one, but one individual NPU repetition does; the supplement correctly discloses this exception. These numbers do not quantify achieved savings, isolate replay from proposal, or bound a redesign changing work/progress. The CPU/NPU 90/90 qualification is reported and flags are retained, but complete original 358H ID arrays are not available for me to requalify from the processed cost CSV.

**M4 shows limited measured two-mode opportunity and an unsuccessful incremental guard.** I reconstruct all 540 cost observations into 60 three-condition prompt medians and exactly match every original median. Guard/Always GM latency is 1.02707602, with Guard faster on 24 prompts and Always faster on 36; 55/180 Guard runs fall back. Target beats Always on only two prompts, both Spec-Bench. Always/oracle is 1.0006919671212657, so the descriptive GM reduction is 0.0691488634%. Both exceptional prompts trigger all three Guard fallbacks, yet Guard medians remain above target. Family GMs also favor Always. The oracle chooses only the two measured fixed modes with hindsight. It neither bounds arbitrary within-request switching nor establishes that transition costs caused Guard's loss. The current paper does not claim an adaptive-policy advantage. I did not independently requalify M4 direct IDs from these cost-only observations or rerun its bootstrap/calibration.

## Strongest acceptance and rejection arguments

The strongest acceptance argument is that the paper goes beyond a correctness gate: prospective complete non-reference outputs can be reproduced with fewer execution dependencies. B570's draft-free success and RTX's distinct failed simplifications are falsifiable observations, supported by full-array endpoints rather than informal text comparisons. Negative cost and control results are reported against appropriate comparators, with careful treatment of prompt units, failed gates, secondary analyses, and estimator-specific counterfactuals. This is technically valuable systems evidence, and a research paper need not invent a decoder or show positive speedup to contribute knowledge.

The strongest rejection argument is that the new knowledge remains essentially a finite mapping from specified programs to their outputs. The B570 result is informative, but only four distinct trajectories distinguish the successful predictor from the reference. RTX's successful description retains proposal-conditioned speculative verification and shared numerical routines; its unsuccessful UB2 alternative does not equate actual forward traces. Thus the contrast does not isolate which execution information matters or give a transferable selection principle. The conditional ceilings add useful engineering budgets, but their mathematics is elementary accounting of already measured bottlenecks. M4 evaluates one cumulative-cost rule on a cohort with almost no measured fixed-mode selection opportunity. These results are truthful, yet their demonstrated scientific reach is modest relative to a research-track contribution.

Primary literature already establishes numerical configuration sensitivity, consumer-runtime slowdowns/correctness gates, determinism through shape-consistent verification, and correctness/cost forensics. The distinct contribution here is prospective complete native-output reproduction, not those established phenomena. My public reading supports this distinction; it is not an exhaustive priority audit. In particular, Chordiya's §6.1 directly reports batch-shape argmax changes without speculative machinery, and §6.5 diagnoses ineffective small-batch parallel verification. This makes the finite reproducer result the part that must carry research novelty.

## Validity defects and unresolved issues

I found **no material arithmetic defect, denominator inconsistency, or unsupported necessity/causal claim in the current bounded results**. The retained M4 freeze-hash recording error and V3 endpoint-parser failure are actual historical defects. The manuscript discloses recovery/exclusion rather than silently treating them as clean prospective evidence. I cannot authenticate that recovery, source allowlists, original sealing chronology, or omitted model/runtime/compiler/driver bytes from this compact projection. Content hashes authenticate delivered bytes, not trusted time or original physical execution. This is an evidence-coverage limit, not proof that the reported ordering is false.

RTX V4's fan/host-policy amendment after predictor capture is a substantive scope qualification. It precedes any native live outcome, preserves candidates/endpoints, and is disclosed in supplement Q.2. It means the entire physical host policy was not one unchanged frozen condition across prediction and live phases. The finite observed matches survive that limitation, but phrases about a frozen configuration should not invite a stronger physical-invariance reading. The other unresolved issues—portable mechanism, trace-matched alternative, minimality, and diagnostic utility—limit contribution strength; they are not logically required to make the presently bounded array and budget claims true.

## At most three high-value changes

1. **Existing-record repair:** state the V4 host-policy amendment briefly in the main study-design section, explicitly distinguishing unchanged numerical candidates/configuration from the changed physical host policy. This improves reviewability without new measurements or new claims.
2. **Additional research:** specify a discriminating shape/state/trace-matched contrast on informative divergent cases, with continuation that changes only the intended block when testing its delayed effect. This could determine why one reproduction description succeeds and another fails. It would strengthen scientific inference, not validate a claim the current paper has already bounded correctly.
3. **Additional research:** demonstrate one concrete diagnostic use of the simpler reproducer—isolating a discrepancy or validating a correction—and check the result back on native execution. Measure or otherwise demonstrate the added diagnostic value rather than assuming it from input reduction. This would strengthen practical impact. The closed hardware campaign should remain closed until such a study is separately specified; no new study is requested as a prerequisite for the truth of this packet's finite results.
