# Frozen pilot protocol

The research question is whether local cuts buy complete service time over whole-graph cuts or per-row attention while preserving the scalar reference. A second question is which phases benefit over scalar speculative and ordinary service. We predict fewer physical attention operations for QB than PQ, fewer whole-graph calls for QB than WB at crossing positions, four-column CUDA projections in wide scoped calls, and identical committed f32 bytes for all four methods. Uniformly aligned prefill can give WB and QB identical partitions. Stock batching can change logits even when greedy outputs agree.
