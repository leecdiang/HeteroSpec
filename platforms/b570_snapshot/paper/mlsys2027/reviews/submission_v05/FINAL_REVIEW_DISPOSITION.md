## Accepted limitations

- No new algorithmic contribution, demonstrated Guard benefit, risk reduction, tail protection, threshold optimality, or controller portability.
- Separate historical sessions/workloads, co-varying hardware/runtime/quantization, one narrow model family, and no controlled cross-platform causal ranking.
- Replay and proposal are jointly timed; persistence benefit is unmeasured. The 358H runtime intervention failed eligibility and has no formal R2 performance result.
- Historical text-path success does not certify original generated-ID sequences; lost direct traces cannot be reconstructed from equality counts.
- Prompt intervals condition on campaign, selected policy and observed repetitions; they do not measure full session, thermal or recalibration uncertainty. Prospective evaluation remains one M4 configuration, 60 held-out prompts, three repetitions and a 128-token cap.
- Conditional post-prefill latency excludes loading and initial prefill. Sensor domains do not support common energy rankings; primary M4 package-energy improvement is not established.
- Recovered M4 metadata, limited Windows raw provenance, strongly supported rather than proven 358H source identity, compact-only runtime diagnostic, and the unexecuted corrected synthetic ceiling remain disclosed.

“Accepted limitation” means retained honestly within the closed revision, not scientifically harmless or guaranteed acceptable to a program committee.
