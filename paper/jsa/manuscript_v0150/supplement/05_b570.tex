\subsection{Necessary-state receiver, shams and crosses}
A clean UB512 receiver imports complete source-defined sequence KV with exact positions. It consumes the complete initial 128,256-element FP32 vector through the same stateless native greedy apply/accept semantics, reconstructs the empty generated prefix, and evaluates the first pending output at the correct continuation position. Dynamic logit/output buffers are recreated as needed; opaque graph-cache pointers are not copied. This is a prospectively specified necessary-state boundary contract, not a claim that old public sequence serialization captured every runtime object.

The 16 own-path shams cover four historical inputs, A/B donors and two repeats. Every sham matches original complete output and the admitted raw-state checks with no extra model forward. Only after this gate passes are 40 cross requests interpreted: two affected inputs times four cells times three repeats, plus two stable controls times four cells times two repeats. The two affected tasks satisfy AA=AB=A and BA=BB=B; all cells choose initial top 1 token 1271. Thus the initial-logit swap is a negative control for first greedy sampling. It cannot establish that future logits do not matter or that arbitrary initial-logit perturbations are harmless.

The KV manipulation identifies conditional suffix sensitivity under this receiver. It does not isolate one K/V layer, one token position, a K-only versus V-only mediator or a necessary mechanism in every possible implementation. The stable controls remain output-invariant even with different KV bytes. The result is narrower than full causal identification of every arrow from projection difference to final token, but stronger than merely correlating Prefill hashes with output.

\subsection{Physical work, cost and non-admitted timing}
The previous speculative actor executes a logical seed+proposal verification as two occupied width-one micrographs at $k=1$. The 563 cycles across 11 completed candidate witnesses in the physical-work study retain separate draft proposal/catch-up counts. One API-observer request failed a logging threshold in that earlier study and was not replayed; its partial dispatch metadata is not a completed endpoint or GPU stage timing~\cite{bwork}.

The original stock-relative qualification remains failed. A later independent post-hoc full-output audit~\cite{bpostaudit} supports a distinct production-path pairing across 24 tasks and 240 repetitions: $R_{\rm prod}/C=0.7192637597463166$, $M_{\rm match}/C=0.7279267978304574$, and $R_{\rm prod}/M_{\rm match}=0.988099025740$. These are negative same-output descriptive economics, not newly certified stock-preserving speedups. They were not remeasured in the causal follow-up.

No completed GPU timeline is admitted. Hardware timestamp support is present, but the pinned backend does not expose the absolute queue/query/fence intervals required by the declared timeline contract. No alternative numerical DLL was substituted to force an observation. Continuous physical residency and exclusive or overlapping GPU stage times remain unknown. Original negative paid results and new causal observations retain their separate denominators and interpretation.
