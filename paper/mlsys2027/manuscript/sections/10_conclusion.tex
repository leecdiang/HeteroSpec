\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
Fresh direct-ID studies qualify RTX serialization but leave B570 blocked. On the tested B570 stack, a frozen target-only UB1 predictor achieves 72/72 predictor-to-live complete-ID agreement, demonstrating target-partition sufficiency without a unique numerical cause. On 358H, the state interface consumes an isolated draft-device advantage; its later intervention stops at qualification. Prospective M4 Guard preserves direct-ID output and beats target-only but trails fixed speculation in the paired secondary comparison.
