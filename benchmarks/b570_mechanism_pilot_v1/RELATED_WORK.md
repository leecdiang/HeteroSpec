# Primary-source comparison

| Source / existing principle | Our retained evidence | Unresolved prediction for this pilot |
|---|---|---|
| [LLM-42: Enabling Determinism in LLM Inference with Verified Speculation](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.17768v2): fast dynamic decode with fixed-shape verification/rollback; maintain KV consistency under its deterministic-prefill assumption. | B570 prefill handoff suffices on old inputs but /72 and continuation changes are counterexamples to universal repair. | A natural donor/current-recipe crossing can distinguish inherited state from the immediate verification computation. |
| [Vosti: Specifying, Implementing, and Verifying Deterministic LLM Inference](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.38981v1): fixed model/deployment specification; engine cache provenance, logical-input preservation and relational kernel contracts across scheduling, chunking and reuse. | We have API state roundtrips, real positions/ownership and finite ID qualification, not an engine or Vulkan arithmetic proof. | Aligned prefix, ownership and full sham can support a bounded empirical intervention; they do not verify bitwise numerical invariance of all paths. |
| [Thinking Machines: Defeating Nondeterminism in LLM Inference](https://thinkingmachines.ai/blog/defeating-nondeterminism-in-llm-inference/): batch-invariant reduction paths for RMSNorm, matrix multiplication and attention; shape/slicing-dependent numerical paths can change greedy outputs. | Existing B570 parent traces show recipe-sensitive full trajectories and errors with substantial margins. | Holding an actual logical caller fixed may expose current-recipe score/decision effects, with no presumption of a unique attention kernel. |

Selective determinism, KV consistency, fixed numerical paths and batch-invariant inference are established principles, not new names for a contribution here. Our potential increment is a recoverable, bounded B570/Q8/Vulkan natural-state intervention and, only if mechanism plus costs support it, one task-independent pilot recipe with a strong ordinary-R baseline. Logit change, greedy decision change and restoration of a full trajectory are separate endpoints. Existing RTX results already provide local state crossing and constrained-attention evidence; this platform does not inherit their proofs or deployment benefit.

The cited primary sources were actually read before the new model requests. Source-level eligibility is not measured dispatch, and a finite empirical panel is not a universal invariance theorem. No novelty claim follows from audit-check counts.
