## Retained boundaries and unresolved risks

Natural crosses are local evidence; full counterfactual continuations and general opaque stochastic-state restoration are unavailable. HumanEval crosses test whole recipes. Sustained B570 UB128 continuation is not an isolated-block attribution. Phase minimality is within a finite switch family; broader geometry failures limit extrapolation.

The RTX matched-request effect is small, heterogeneous and reversed in round 2; task bootstrap does not estimate independent-day/session uncertainty. Ordinary-reference paid gain is not speculative-increment gain or a fastest-baseline proof. B570 loses to stronger baselines despite its improvement over old N1; the missing /400 old-N1 payload and interruption time remain unknown. Fixed-UB costs use saved task ratios rather than a new raw timing parse. Historical text, direct-ID, energy and timer contracts stay distinct.

The 358H saved ceilings/budgets are conditional arithmetic, not achieved optimization or NPU impossibility. The original-host 180-row prompt-ceiling audit is inherited; summary/source checks do not reopen it. Source-byte authentication does not imply a fresh line-by-line audit of all files or every private raw payload/model/driver binary.

Single-Qwen3-family evidence, absent same-contract external method comparisons and unmeasured independent-session stability remain substantive scientific risks. Primary-source related-work positioning was checked without adding references or claiming superiority. Author identity/declarations, permanent access and final bibliography status stay pending.

No model request, forward, warmup, hardware experiment, PR merge, submission or public data release was performed. Local rebuilds do not establish live Overleaf or Editorial Manager acceptance. Inherited `validation/INHERITED_*` records retain their earlier attribution.
