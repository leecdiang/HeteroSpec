- License: MIT; saved as `humanizer.LICENSE`.

Application to the final manuscript: read the complete current draft, identify repetitive rhetorical contrast, objections no reader has raised, redundant concluding sentences, stock transitions, vague authority, and piled-up qualifications. Rewrite these into direct scientific statements. Preserve real comparisons, negative findings, necessary qualifications, names, numbers, dates, citations, and causal direction. Do not invent anecdotes, opinions, stronger effects, or a new authorial personality. A separate fidelity pass must compare the rewritten prose with the evidence before acceptance.

Historical prescriptions that conflict with contemporary scientific English are not imposed. Required scientific negation and calibrated uncertainty remain intact.
