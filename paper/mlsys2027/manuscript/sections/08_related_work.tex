\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}
\paragraph{Performance and heterogeneous execution.}
Speculative decoding amortizes target computation under its algorithmic assumptions \citep{leviathan2023}. Prior work already documents verification and workload limits: \citet{illusion} study verification dominance and batch effects in vLLM; \citet{specllm} account for conditional execution cost and runtime control; \citet{distance} derive edge--cloud profitability conditions. On consumer Apple silicon, \citet{lossless} report gains and slowdowns across five backend configurations, diagnose ineffective batch-parallel verification in one quantized Metal path, test output agreement, and observe target-only batch-shape argmax changes. Slowdown and correctness gating are therefore established background. Heterogeneous placement is also established: Dovetail, DuoDecoding, and Mirror-SD study GPU/CPU or GPU/NPU paths \citep{dovetail,duodecoding,mirror}; \citet{compilerassisted} combine an analytical cost model with compiler-oriented CPU/GPU partitioning, and Lever jointly optimizes draft, verification, and CPU/NPU execution for a smartphone target \citep{lever}. State reuse has prior art in compressed draft KV memory \citep{compactmemory}. Our 358H evidence instead accounts for one measured, combined replay-plus-proposal interface; it does not claim a general placement or persistence result.

\paragraph{Numerical paths and output contracts.}
\citet{batchcorrect} distinguish ragged-batch synchronization errors from residual numerical mismatches. \citet{numerics} study output variation across system configurations, and LLM-42 addresses deterministic inference through verified speculation \citep{llm42}. Numerical path dependence is not new here. Our distinct test prospectively qualifies specific RTX/B570 paths against captured generated IDs, separately from historical text-path timing. For B570, H1 was selected after 36/36 exposed discovery matches, then complete predictions were frozen before 72 reserved live executions; four prompts could have contradicted a reference-echo explanation and instead matched the non-reference prediction in all 12 runs. H2 was unrun in that original B570 tree. Subsequent same-state B570 forks test sampled immediate and continuation effects separately; RTX's failed H1 and independently frozen P/R replay study supply a different program-level prospective test on shared numerical routines. This tests path admissibility and predictive sufficiency, not a new numerical phenomenon, unique kernel cause, or competing reserved predictor.

\paragraph{Adaptive speculation and the study boundary.}
TurboSpec adjusts speculation for serving goodput \citep{turbospec}; BanditSpec selects configurations online \citep{banditspec}; ACL HeteroSpec adapts verification from contextual entropy \citep{aclheterospec}. Adaptive control is established prior work. Our M4 Guard is a qualified runtime control test, not a new policy advantage: it beats target-only, but fixed speculation is faster in the post-study paired secondary comparison. The paper's central evidence is prospective direct-ID qualification, bounded frozen trajectory prediction and intervention, and same-path realized-cost evaluation.
