The v0.5 decision primarily rejected modest scientific payoff after claim
corrections, rather than demonstrated invalidity. v0.7 genuinely changes its
evidentiary basis: the qualification core has direct observations and a common
protocol family. It does not create a uniform strict-contract performance study.
The missing paired M4 contrast is resolved against Guard. A fresh Weak Reject
therefore has a different, narrower rationale: limited explanatory reach despite
stronger validity. It must not repeat the obsolete claim that a direct-ID RTX
serialized positive control or paired Guard interval is absent.

The fresh mock recommendation is **Weak Reject, moderate confidence (3/5)**.
The rigor review supports Weak Accept from narrow validity, and the champion
supports Weak Accept for empirical characterization. Systems/adversarial/novelty
give reasoned Weak Reject assessments. Writing and visual find no delivery
blockers but do not settle significance. No numerical vote average or automatic
carryover determines the mock verdict.

The minimum missing scientific contribution is a validated transferable
qualification consequence or a controlled explanatory/predictive result. A
successful speed remedy is one possible route, not a mandatory requirement.
The current title remains defensible; reviewers do not establish consensus for
replacement. Manuscript and evidence remain frozen for author review, and all
future recommendations are unexecuted.
