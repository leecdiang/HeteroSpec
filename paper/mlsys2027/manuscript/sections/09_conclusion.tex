\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
Under the chosen strict greedy-reference contract, consumer speculative decoding spans profitable exact, exact-negative, correctness-inadmissible, and state-interface-bound regimes. The physically cheapest target-forward path may be excluded by correctness, and isolated accelerator throughput may fail to represent the stateful draft cost paid in the speculative loop. Retaining both M4 workloads, the blocked and serialized verification controls, and the complete 358H decomposition makes this a coherent hardware characterization without a new runtime result. The hardware-study conclusion is stable; the final paper conclusion remains provisional pending a separately measured software contribution or the decision to remove that reserved scope.
