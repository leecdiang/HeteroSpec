\section{Design Implications and Limits}
\label{sec:implications}
\subsection{Constrain the transaction where evidence warrants it}
A current-path effect motivates controlling verification geometry; a retained-state effect motivates preserving state formation or changing its interface; an interaction requires checking both. The RTX crosses motivate target-phase attention constraints, whereas B570's distinct phase binding controls a whole recipe. These local observations narrow the design space without uniquely identifying a kernel or implementing an automatic selector.

The qualification sequence matters. Own-path shams test restoration fidelity, local crosses test aligned boundaries, and complete registered outputs test the candidate. B570's immediate null followed by trajectory changes makes the last step indispensable. Locality also does not guarantee low cost: mask 3 constrains fewer phases than mask 15, yet misses the extra generation-speed gate.

\subsection{Use both paid baselines for operating decisions}
Ordinary $R/C$ measures a deployment change, matched $M/C$ the increment from adding a draft. RTX's large ordinary-request improvement therefore includes a draft-free target-path opportunity; the remaining speculative increment is small and uneven. A faster qualified ordinary implementation could narrow the deployment benefit, since $R$ is a paid reference rather than a proven fastest decoder.

The failures change concrete choices: retain the frozen RTX anchor rather than its slower qualified wider draft; reject the B570 recipe for latency against both strong baselines; avoid adding the measured M4 guard on an assumed control benefit; and address 358H target work or its state interface before expecting draft-only optimization to reach parity. These decisions use each campaign's contract rather than a shared platform ranking.

\subsection{Scope of the evidence}
Repair evidence covers one Qwen3 target/draft family, finite panels and pinned runtime variants. The RTX project-unused panel is a selected 24-input MBPP panel; unused means absent from accessible project execution records, not unseen during training. Geometry failures require renewed qualification after configuration changes. The results establish neither cross-family transfer nor reference preservation on arbitrary inputs.

Discrete output agreement is not numerical equivalence. The score/KV witnesses do not separate every reduction, precision, layout or scheduling effect; shams cannot exhaust hidden backend state. Selected cuts and a four-switch family cannot establish universal minimality or superiority over unimplemented prior methods.

Task medians and descriptive intervals do not eliminate session, thermal, ordering or infrastructure effects. B570's missing old-N1 payload and unknown raw comparisons remain explicit. Timers and historical energy domains differ, and later direct-ID qualification cannot certify earlier text-based costs. The author-side CPU reconstruction covers included sources and exports, with inherited private-archive audits identified separately (Supplement \S S7).
