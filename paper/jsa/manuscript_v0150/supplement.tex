\begin{abstract}
This supplement specifies evidence contracts, complete reported comparison tables, limitations of the operator and retained-state interventions, and the disposition of the historical experiment ledger. It keeps numerical correspondence, complete-output qualification, runtime cost and causal scope separate. Reported-value consistency checks performed for this reconstruction are not a new GPU reproduction or a rerun of the original hosts' full raw-data audits. Commit-pinned source records and an integration guide are included in the author package.
\end{abstract}
