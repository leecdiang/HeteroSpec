\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}
\paragraph{Performance and cost accounting.}
Speculative decoding amortizes target computation while preserving a target sampling distribution under its algorithmic assumptions \citep{leviathan2023}. \citet{illusion} study verification dominance and workload/batch effects in vLLM; \citet{specllm} control runtime policies and account for committed work and conditional execution cost. \citet{distance} derive edge--cloud profitability conditions. On consumer Apple silicon, \citet{lossless} reports positive and negative regimes across five backend configurations, with three decelerating, and diagnose ineffective batch-parallel verification in a quantized Metal configuration. The study also tests distribution agreement and greedy equality, and reports target-only batch-shape argmax changes. Slowdown, latency-gap profitability, numerical path dependence, and correctness gating are therefore close prior art. Our distinction is prospective direct generated-ID qualification on RTX/B570, followed by a B570 target-partition hypothesis frozen before reserved inference and a 72/72 complete serialized-sequence prediction. We separate physical opportunity from admissibility under the output contract.

\paragraph{Heterogeneous execution.}
Dovetail places drafting on a GPU and verification on a CPU \citep{dovetail}; DuoDecoding uses hardware-aware CPU/GPU draft budgets \citep{duodecoding}; Mirror-SD considers complementary GPU/NPU pipelines \citep{mirror}. \citet{compilerassisted} combine an analytical cost model with compiler-oriented partitioning, predicting profitable CPU/GPU operating points validated on Cortex-A/Mali hardware. Lever jointly optimizes drafting, verification, and CPU/NPU execution for a DRAM-resident draft and flash-backed smartphone target \citep{lever}. These systems establish heterogeneous placement and predictive performance modeling. Our B570 predictor instead concerns measured output trajectories and qualification. Our 358H result measures loss of an isolated draft-device advantage through one realized state interface; it does not contradict successful heterogeneous co-design such as Lever.

\paragraph{Adaptive and contextual speculation.}
TurboSpec profiles execution and adjusts or disables speculation for serving goodput \citep{turbospec}. BanditSpec selects configurations online with stopping-time regret analysis \citep{banditspec}; our oracle ratio remains descriptive. ACL HeteroSpec estimates verification complexity from entropy and adapts depth and pruning to contextual heterogeneity \citep{aclheterospec}. Context-adaptive verification allocation is established. Our B570 predictor is a mechanism diagnostic for qualification, not an adaptive decoding policy. M4 supplies a runtime endpoint without incremental Guard benefit or serving-controller comparison.

\paragraph{Correctness and numerical reproducibility.}
\citet{batchcorrect} distinguish ragged-batch synchronization errors from residual numerical mismatches, revising their earlier \emph{Batch Speculative Decoding Done Right} preprint. \citet{numerics} study numerical output variation across system configurations; LLM-42 enforces deterministic inference through verified speculation \citep{llm42}. Numerical path dependence and deterministic speculative inference are established. Our contribution concerns their systems consequence for execution-path admissibility and a prospectively frozen target-partition prediction, rather than a new numerical phenomenon or synchronization diagnosis.

\paragraph{State and memory reuse.}
\citet{compactmemory} maintain incrementally updated compressed draft KV memory for long contexts. Such designs differ from our measured full-prefix replay interface, but establish state reuse as prior art. The 358H evidence identifies realized replay-plus-proposal cost; its later intervention stops at qualification. Neither it nor the M4 control result measures a persistence benefit.
