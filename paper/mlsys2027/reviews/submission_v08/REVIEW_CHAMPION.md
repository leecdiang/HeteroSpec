Target-only UB1 matches 36/36 exposed discovery trajectories, is frozen, and predicts every one of 72 reserved complete serialized trajectories, including four divergent prompts. Predicting blocked 60/72 reference agreement before live outcomes is a useful negative result. Live drafting is unnecessary for the observed path difference in this tested stack.

This is not just advising practitioners to test the path. It gives a controlled next test after failure: compare target execution partitions before changing the draft model or hardware placement. The paper then shows why correctness admission and realized profitability remain separate through 358H stateful costs and the M4 Guard/Always contrast. A systems characterization can be valuable without a new speedup algorithm.

The case is bounded. H1 is one B570 stack, model pair, greedy k=2 and HumanEval reserved sample. RTX does not share a demonstrated mechanism; M4 Guard has no incremental latency benefit; historical text equality does not become direct-ID evidence. These limits are visible in the paper. I would support acceptance for a measured, prospective explanatory contribution while asking authors to keep the release claim no broader than the PDF.
