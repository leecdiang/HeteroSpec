\section{Reproduction, disclosure and claim audit}
\label{supp:reproduction}
\subsection{Local reproduction and source access}
The package supplies editable manuscript/figure sources, 21 cost comparisons, reported task/session medians, evidence-scope JSON, the 28-group ledger, source pointers and build/check scripts. Building requires no model or GPU and leaves experiment records unchanged. Checks cover counts, regression denominators, ratio identities and limitations, including recomputation of 21 aggregate and 42 session ratios. They do not audit native timing records.

An additional CPU audit restored six pinned primary archives (638 parts; 52,925 indexed logical paths, including inherited copies), verified their archived bytes and reran the saved checks and analyses. The final B570 audit reproduced 10 artifacts and five inherited PR99 artifacts and passed its 15 rejection controls. External model weights were not rehashed, and no inference was rerun. The early and final B570 auditors required only a Windows-path separator adapter; their source and evidence bytes were unchanged. Recorded differences are limited to skipped model-check metadata, manifest path ordering and floating-point aggregate roundoff below $10^{-12}$. The separate PR94 cross-audit was not rerun, and the archive checks cannot recover state payloads that the original observer did not save. Public reproduction still requires access to the underlying records, source capsules and exact model assets; the availability statement must retain any restrictions.
