# B570 natural prefix reuse: complete native result

All four policies passed native qualification on all32 held-out conversation roots. The complete four-session experiment does **not** demonstrate AUTO service benefit: all three family-adjusted intervals include1, and the predeclared3% engineering screen fails. AUTO is the frozen constant WB-rechunk policy. QB has the lowest descriptive geometric service time; this does not establish its statistical superiority.

Protocol disposition: **useful fixed policy but no selector benefit**, where usefulness means a functioning, scalar-compatible fixed deployment, not a demonstrated speedup over another fixed arm. There is no equivalence claim, TTFT benefit claim, speculative benefit claim or positive TPDS application-benefit claim from this result.
