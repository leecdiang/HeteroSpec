\section{Selecting a reference-qualified implementation}
\label{sec:decisionrule}
The experiments support three ordered choices for the declared workload: qualify the repair, decide whether to draft, and select the local representation (Table~\ref{tab:deploymentrule}). Keeping these choices separate assigns each measured gain to its actual source.

\label{tab:deploymentrule}

Choice & Evidence required & Resulting decision\\\midrule

Draft on or off & Target-only and speculative requests with the same repair, reference and timer & Use the cheaper qualified policy; local repair changes this choice for Qwen3/Llama, while SmolLM2 favors target-only execution on the original panel.\\

\paragraph{Repair scope is a scheduling decision}
The matched seven-arm panel shows that local repair changes the sign of the draft increment for two families. Both PQ and QB make that transition by retaining batched projections and feed-forward work. QB then reduces the representation cost within local repair. Target-only batching supplies a useful endpoint even when the draft adds cost, as SmolLM2 demonstrates.

\paragraph{Numerical organization belongs in runtime interfaces}
Paired Q/mask views carry query and head semantics; the phase flag and layout-sensitive cache check preserve those choices during graph reuse. The scoped Vulkan port retains the noncontiguous AV shader while exposing independent query workgroups~\cite{b570gqarecords}. Producer-state qualification completes this interface: endpoint-equivalent states can contain noninterchangeable KV components. Together these results identify the state, layout and evaluation properties a portable implementation must preserve.

\paragraph{Optimize the complete service transaction}
The matched Qwen3 study establishes practical QB benefits at $k=3$ with resident and fresh contexts~\cite{rtxnativebridge}. The original $k=1$ panels, compact-QH transactions and B570 prefill-submission study identify cases where reduced local work does not pass a complete-cost screen. The implementation choice therefore uses measured request cost under the intended draft length, backend and lifecycle.

These control points complement batch-invariant kernels~\cite{thinkingmachines}, reproducible accumulation~\cite{reproblas} and phase-aware tiled execution~\cite{spectra}. They also give a concrete hardware-design requirement: preserve the declared per-query evaluation, including matrix-instruction behavior beyond dtype~\cite{ptx}, while exposing independent work across queries. Hardware performance would require an equal-resource comparison on the request-critical path; the archived same-policy AV model supplies an evaluation-parity control~\cite{studyrecords}.
