\section{Experimental design}
\label{sec:design}
The evidence consists of experiments with different roles; counts are not pooled as if they represented independent inputs or dates.

\begin{tabularx}{\columnwidth}{@{}p{.25\columnwidth}Y@{}}
\toprule
Study & Question answered \\
\midrule
CUDA confirmation & Complete registered outputs across three model families \\

The original CUDA experiments use pinned Q8_0 target/draft pairs for Qwen3, SmolLM2 and Llama, F16 KV, non-flash attention and the registered phase policy. The six-session replication uses fresh workers and counterbalanced PQ/QB blocks. The later RTX mapping experiments use their own fixed synthetic geometries and cost windows; they do not inherit request-level conclusions automatically.

B570 experiments use a separate Vulkan frontend and Llama-family quantized runtime for state and backend studies. The latest request panels use Qwen3-8B Q8_0 target and Qwen3-0.6B Q8_0 draft. The B570 and RTX closure panels have separate prospective references, service definitions, context policies and device environments. Their ratios answer within-panel questions; they must not be pooled into a cross-device speedup.

All studies retain failed or weak outcomes and use fixed input panels. Repeated requests and fresh processes improve measurement coverage but do not turn four exposed inputs into a population sample. The later RTX closure uses one machine/date and $k=1$; its conclusions are limited to that policy and panel.
