\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
Favorable local indicators do not by themselves establish gains in integrated post-prefill decode on the tested consumer configurations. M4 exposes exact positive and negative workload-conditioned regimes. Path controls show that correctness can remove cheap verification, while 358H measurements show that an isolated draft advantage need not survive stateful integration. These bounded diagnoses motivate qualification before cost control. The 358H runtime intervention stops at qualification; the separately frozen M4 guard preserves exact outputs and achieves profitable held-out execution against target-only, although fixed speculation has a higher aggregate point estimate. Together, the studies connect consumer-stack characterization to a measured runtime decision process whose usefulness depends on both the admitted path and its realized cost.
