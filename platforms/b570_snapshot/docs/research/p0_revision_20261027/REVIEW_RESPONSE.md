## Limits that remain deliberately visible

- E2 is retrospective, uses six sessions on one host over two dates, and lacks GS. Pointwise t5 intervals require session-level assumptions and do not replace the registered PQ/QB intervals.
- The work audit establishes equality of recorded logical/decision/histogram fields and endpoints, not equality of full cycle trace order, bitwise KV state, logits, or per-operator time.
- Original arm workers were not temporally interleaved at the seven-arm group level. Matching cannot remove unobserved drift.
- Historical runtime freezes and executable identities differ in a documented subset. GS/QB share the original executable; metadata4 is limited to specified Smol/Llama PQ workers. No identity is retroactively relabeled.
- The Llama new-input failure, unprofitable Smol drafting, small/uncertain QB increments and k1 failures are retained.
- The next experiments may produce failures, no trigger, or no profit. Their value is to resolve bounded claims; a favorable outcome is not a stopping requirement.

The final decision is **substantive enhancement, with no unresolved mandatory P0 finding**. It is not a prediction of journal acceptance or proof that the paper now has universal platform/input coverage.
