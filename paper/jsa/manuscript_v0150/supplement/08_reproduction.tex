\section{Reproduction, disclosure and claim audit}
\label{supp:reproduction}
\subsection{Local reproduction and source access}
The package provides LaTeX sources, editable vector figures, the 21-cell cost table, reported task/session medians, evidence-scope JSON, the 28-group ledger, source pointers, and build/check scripts. Building requires no model or GPU and leaves experimental records unchanged. The checker verifies counts, regression denominators, ratio identities and stated limitations, and recomputes 21 aggregate and 42 session ratios from reported medians. It does not audit native timing records.

The original-host audits separately reconcile native results and tensor bytes, run standard-library checks and malformed-evidence tests, and restore local/authenticated GitHub archives. The final B570 report records 15 rejection controls and reconstruction of 10 new artifacts plus five PR99 artifacts. These are attributed source results, not audits repeated in this revision. Public reproduction also requires access to the underlying records, source capsules and exact model assets; any restrictions must remain in the availability statement.

\subsection{Claim scope and historical records}
\kw{CLAIM\_EVIDENCE.md} binds each claim to its source, evidence level and limits: mathematical indexing, sampled CUDA correspondence, finite complete-output qualification, whole-request repair cost, or KV sensitivity under a validated receiver. These properties remain distinct.

Later confirmation supersedes the historical Qwen-only scope without rewriting archived metadata. Stronger causal evidence does not repair the earlier failed factorial or stock-reference qualification, even where post-hoc outputs agree. Each campaign retains its source identity.

\kw{AUTHOR\_CHECKLIST.md} records the remaining source-lowering, PQ-capacity, replay and receiver reviews, author declarations and journal checks. The title avoids the unrelated ACL HeteroSpec name~\cite{aclheterospec}. No new model experiment, journal submission, arXiv update or PR merge accompanies this revision.
