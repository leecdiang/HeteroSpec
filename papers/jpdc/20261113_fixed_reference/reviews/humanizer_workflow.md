## Editing rules applied

The related-work section organizes methods by the reference they preserve and the state they constrain. It avoids a list of paper summaries followed by an unsupported novelty claim. Technical distinctions precede performance rhetoric.

The prose uses specific actions: reconstruct arithmetic, choose kernels, retain cache entries, replace KV state, split query groups. Broad promotional adjectives, stock transitions, repeated warnings, and claims about how the document was assembled were removed. Necessary conditions remain where they distinguish scientific guarantees. For example, it is material that LLM-42 repairs KV state and that our argument is conditional rather than a mechanized whole-engine proof.

Language cleanup does not change numbers, baseline names, citation keys, model/deployment labels, qualifications, uncertainty, or the status of measurements. It does not add invented experience, asserted production demand, a novel-first claim, or a universal speedup.
