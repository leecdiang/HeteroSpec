# Stage decomposition decision — v0.10.0

**Do not start or recommend a2×2×2stage study for this manuscript now.** All three independent initial whole-paper reviews evaluated the exact new manuscript openly. None identified absence of unique stage attribution as a high-priority logical blocker of the finite prediction/paired-output claim. The systems recheck confirms this after operational methods and study boundaries were clarified. The remaining concerns are incremental systems knowledge/usefulness, limited generality of new changed paths, and inspectable provenance; repeating or enlarging stage cells does not itself demonstrate a reusable description-construction method or a useful engineering capability.

RTX target-continuation UB2→UB1 did not satisfy its frozen reference-recovery/no-new-regression target; historical globalUB1 also varies prefill/draft and effective threads. These facts motivate hypotheses but do not identify stage necessity or an interaction. The manuscript states the controlled outcome without using it to prove a cause. B570's intervention includes sample/state handoff and exposed diagnostics, so cross-platform difference is not an isolated hardware/stage comparison.

The paper should remain a bounded prospective execution-description/intervention case study with independent qualification/cost evidence. Stop adding model experiments; finish author copyediting, artifact accessibility and venue/novelty positioning. This recommendation is based on the exact independent reviews, not on a desired positive result. The final rechecks preserve original judgments rather than requesting new scores.

A future stage study could be justified by a separately stated causal question about competing explanations, with its own descriptions,8-cell qualifications/shams, frozen controls, information contracts, ordering and fixed budget. That would answer a new question; finding an all-matching configuration is not an appropriate stopping rule. No such design or cell is launched in this task, and no one-page proposal is produced because the prompt's high-priority core-blocker condition is not met.
