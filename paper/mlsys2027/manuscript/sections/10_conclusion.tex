\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
On the tested B570 stack, frozen draft-free UB1 prediction matches 72/72 reserved capped sequences, including 12 runs of four reference-divergent prompts; reference/live is 60/72. This advances an observed path sensitivity to a falsifiable full-trajectory prediction, while leaving lossless compatibility blocked. Same-state forks and independently orchestrated RTX replay constrain different explanatory questions. Cost then supplies concrete decisions: qualified RTX serialization is slower in its instrumented boundary; fixed-progress 358H target work prevents parity even at zero draft/residual cost; M4 Guard loses to fixed speculation despite preserving IDs. The result is bounded systems diagnosis and comparator-specific engineering guidance, not a universal mechanism or successful portable optimization.
