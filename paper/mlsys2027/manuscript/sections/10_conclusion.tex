\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
Frozen B570 target-only predictions reproduce 72/72 capped outputs, including \DiagBNonref\ non-reference positions on four distinct trajectories. RTX rejects its specified UB2 description while proposal-conditioned replay matches all 57 outputs. These results identify tested reproduction inputs and removable dependencies, without establishing necessary history or a unique cause; the separate B570 fork leaves immediate causal restoration unsupported. Optimization requires another test: RTX serialization qualifies but is slower, 358H overhead-only ceilings remain below parity, and M4 Guard loses where the two measured fixed modes offer little selection opportunity. Complete prediction, reference compatibility and cost budgets each support a different engineering decision.
