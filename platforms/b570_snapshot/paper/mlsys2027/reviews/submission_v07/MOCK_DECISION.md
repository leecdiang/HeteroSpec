## Contribution significance and explicit decision questions

**Does the new replication change the principal v0.5 Weak Reject reason?** It
weakens important evidentiary objections and strengthens the empirical increment,
but does not overturn the principal limited-payoff objection under this mock
committee's selective general MLSys bar. This conclusion is newly assessed from
the actual stronger evidence, not inherited from the earlier verdict.

**Does an anecdotal observation become a sufficiently consequential replicated
systems finding?** It becomes a credible bounded replicated finding. The shared
tasks, models, greedy k=2, repetitions and all-pairs gate give the qualification
question genuine common structure. Whether that is sufficient is the substantive
disagreement: champion and rigor support weak acceptance; systems, adversarial
and novelty find its reach too narrow. This decision weighs their explanations
and consequences rather than averaging scores.

**Is this now a qualification-boundary study rather than heterogeneous cases?**
The best description is heterogeneous hardware/runtime characterization with a
reproducible prospective qualification-boundary core. Two GPU studies replicate
the question, while the M4 and 358H cases remain distinct. Rebranding all four
stacks as one controlled campaign would be false.

**What major acceptance blocker remains?** The paper does not explain or predict
qualification beyond its tested configuration family. B570 reference/frontend
differences leave a credible alternative explanation for residual serialized
failures. The historical mechanism capture does not isolate the new mechanism.
The 358H evidence usefully bounds complete cost and stateful integration but
does not establish a persistence remedy. Guard adds no demonstrated incremental
utility. These are limits to explanatory consequence, not reasons to require a
new controller or reject every negative characterization paper.

The minimum missing scientific contribution is a validated transferable
consequence: for example, a controlled explanation yielding a falsifiable
qualification prediction on a reserved configuration, or a qualification-
preserving intervention whose practical consequence is measured in the same
campaign. A faster algorithm is not mandatory. Additional repetitions of the
same deterministic finite gate or rhetorical strengthening alone would not
close the significance gap. These are future recommendations, not experiments
authorized by this integration.
