# MLSys 2027 Research Track policy — v0.8.1

Access date: 2026-09-29 (Asia/Shanghai). Official live source: https://mlsys.org/Conferences/2027/CallForResearchPapers . Checked current page, rather than using a cached repository description.

- Double-blind research review; anonymize author identities, affiliations and explicit/implicit identifying references.
- Two columns using the linked official 2025 style, retained by the 2027 CFP; body at most 10 pages, references excluded.
- Every reference explicitly lists every author.
- Appendix is a separate upload after the main paper; no appendix page limit; same deadline as main; reviewers need not read it.
- Paper deadline: **2026-10-30 20:00 UTC** (**2026-10-31 04:00 Asia/Shanghai**). Submission opens 2026-10-10 20:00 UTC.
- All authors need complete active OpenReview profiles with affiliations, conflicts and publication history; create profiles at least two weeks before submission. Register all conflicts on the submission site; undeclared or false conflicts may cause rejection.
- Submission site: https://openreview.net/group?id=MLSys.org/2027/Conference . More process details are still forthcoming on the CFP; no future instructions inferred.

The official page also requires original eligible work and author responsibility for content. This revision is an author-review candidate; account/profile/conflict completion and final title are author actions that cannot be verified from these anonymous files. No submission or conference communication is performed by this PR.
